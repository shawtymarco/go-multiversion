# Minecraft 1.21.5x wire audit

Protocol 766 is locked to gophertunnel `ecff04b74c9d14ae88a284bc45fc9bb180b79853` and converts directly to native protocol 2169. The adapter restores the target packet pools and layouts, including StartGame's item table, ungrouped creative content, raw network-NBT biome definitions, legacy item components, PlayerMovementSettings, camera groups, and pre-1.21.60 packet tail.

Both target packet pools have generated zero-value fixtures. Populated inventory, StartGame, chunk, command, entity, camera, resource-pack, skin, and Text fixtures are decoded and byte-identically re-encoded by an executable built against the locked historical module. Text type 11 is intentionally tolerated so injected client display hotkeys reach the server's non-chat ignore policy instead of terminating the session.

ModalFormRequest and ServerSettingsResponse keep their historical wire layout but carry versioned JSON. Protocol 766 converts current menu `elements` into the required `buttons` array and removes or substitutes current-only custom-form elements while preserving response indexes.
