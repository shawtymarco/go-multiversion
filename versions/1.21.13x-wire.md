# Minecraft 1.21.130-1.21.132 wire audit

Protocol 898 uses final historical gophertunnel
`c839e607304fcd241d3dcd80f3055a510d9c0b3f`; native is protocol 2169 at
`7f058e5ddc393eaa0480dae338c5eee2feb323e6`. The family retains RakNet v11,
`RequestNetworkSettings`, modern compression framing, and native encryption.

In addition to the protocol-924 boundaries, protocol 898 restores the legacy
Text category constant table, byte-sized BookEdit fields, StartGame telemetry
ordering without ServerJoinInformation, mandatory debug-drawer dimension,
the older camera spline vector model, biome data without VillageType, and
diagnostics without memory-category entries. It removes the data-driven UI,
texture-shift, VoxelShapes, CameraSpline, and aim-assist-priority packets added
in protocol 924. Graphics parameters and level sounds are capped at the exact
protocol-898 enums.

The exact historical Reader/Writer passes both pools and all populated
fixtures in both directions. The configured furnace payload also round-trips
byte-identically at 8,181 bytes with SHA256
`99d5901762d56416997ae8a267d7ca42eb6cecb631a14805e0893e282f4c9074`.
