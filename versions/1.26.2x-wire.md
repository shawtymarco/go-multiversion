# Minecraft 1.26.2x wire audit

## Scope and authority

This audit covers stable Minecraft 1.26.20, 1.26.21, and 1.26.23 under
protocol 975. The canonical target is the final historical gophertunnel
protocol-975 snapshot `de090aef4b59e3fb05b595cd20821aecff7729f6`;
the native model is
`7f058e5ddc393eaa0480dae338c5eee2feb323e6`.

Mojang `r/26_u2` at
`436df94790d4ed1132a4db2985962c281f63eda3` and Endstone `r26_u2` at
`a654ca1b319551fc0731ab0c6189fa1377a3eefa` independently confirm
network version 975. No stable patch-internal wire branch was identified, so
1.26.23 is the canonical family version.

## Implemented differences

- The packet pools remove post-975 sound/party-cookie packets,
  `ServerPlayerPostMovePosition`, and client-originating `UpdateBlock`.
- Numeric protocol-975 level-sound IDs map directly to the current string
  sound model. The complete 564-entry historical table, including gaps and
  `Undefined = 601`, is generated from the locked source.
- Boss events, cache status, graphics overrides, inventory content and
  transactions, mob armour, diagnostics, data-driven screen close,
  `StartGame`, `SubChunkRequest`, and the changed shared item layouts use
  their exact protocol-975 field order and widths.
- Attribute layers omit the later noise-name and local-transition fields.
  Biome noise-gradient data translates between the protocol-975 block-list
  model and the native noise-specifier model without changing the input.
- Data-store list/double values, post-975 movement effects, and new primitive
  shapes are filtered because protocol 975 has no faithful representation.
  New sound names fall back explicitly to the historical undefined event.
- `PlayerList` and `SetScore` mixed operations are split, and
  `StartGame` is cloned before selecting the exact stable login version.

Registry, gameplay, and chunk conversion are configured separately through
the registry-aware constructor. Protocol 975 therefore remains absent from
the parameterless `Protocols()` catalogue.

## Verification

The focused suite checks pool identity, both conversion directions, input
non-mutation, unsupported-field filtering, populated boundary values, and
mixed-operation splitting. With `V975_WIRE_ORACLE` pointing to a helper
built from historical gophertunnel `de090aef`, every valid zero-value packet
in both historical pools and all populated high-risk fixtures are decoded and
byte-identically re-encoded by the exact historical Reader and Writer.
